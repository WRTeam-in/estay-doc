# Tax Configuration

Configure the taxes applicable to your property bookings based on your local regulations. You must set up at least one active tax to complete this step.

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Tax Management

In your admin dashboard, navigate to **Location Policies > Taxes** using the left sidebar.

![Tax Management Page](/images/panel/taxstep1.png)

---

## Step 2: Add a New Tax

Click the **Add New Tax** button located at the top right of the page. A modal window will appear.

![Add New Tax Modal](/images/panel/taxstep2.png)

---

## Step 3: Fill in Tax Details

Fill out the tax configuration form with the following information:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tax Name** | Enter a descriptive name for the tax (e.g., "VAT", "City Tax", "GST"). |
| **Description** | Provide a brief explanation of what this tax is for. |
| **Calculation Type** | Choose how the tax is calculated: <br/> - **Percentage**: Calculates a percentage of the booking total. <br/> - **Fixed**: Adds a flat monetary amount to the booking. |
| **Rate Value** | Enter the value. If "Percentage" is selected, enter a number up to 100. If "Fixed" is selected, enter the flat amount in your currency. |
| **Status** | Choose whether this tax should be **Active** (applied to new bookings) or **Inactive**. |

![Tax Form Details](/images/panel/taxstep3.png)

---

## Step 4: Save the Tax

Click the **Save Tax** button at the bottom of the modal. The tax will now be applied to all future property bookings according to its configuration.

:::warning
You cannot delete the last remaining tax if it is the only one in the system. The platform requires at least one tax configuration to exist.
:::
