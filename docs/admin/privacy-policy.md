# Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy page lets you define and publish your platform's data usage and user rights policy. Once saved, it is visible under the **Privacy Policy** link in both the mobile app and web frontend.

## Accessing Privacy Policy Settings

Navigate to **Settings → Privacy Policy** in the admin sidebar.

![Privacy Policy Editor](/images/panel/privacy-policy.png)

## Editing the Privacy Policy

The editor supports full **rich text formatting** including headings, bold, italic, lists, links, and raw HTML.

### Steps to update

1. Go to **Settings → Privacy Policy**.
2. Click inside the editor area.
3. Write or paste your policy content.
4. Use the toolbar to format text (headings, lists, links, etc.).
5. Click **Save** to publish changes immediately.

:::info
The privacy policy is publicly accessible without login. Changes take effect as soon as you save.
:::

## HTML Support

The editor accepts raw HTML. Switch to the HTML source view to paste pre-formatted legal content directly.

```html
<h2>Information We Collect</h2>
<p>We collect information you provide when registering an account...</p>

<h2>How We Use Your Information</h2>
<p>Your information is used to provide and improve our services...</p>
```

:::warning
Review your privacy policy with a legal professional to ensure compliance with applicable regulations such as GDPR and CCPA.
:::
