# Terms & Conditions

The Terms & Conditions page lets you define and publish the legal agreement between your platform and its users. Once saved, it is visible under the **Terms & Conditions** link in both the mobile app and web frontend.

## Accessing Terms & Conditions Settings

Navigate to **Settings → Terms & Conditions** in the admin sidebar.

![Terms & Conditions Editor](/images/panel/terms-conditions.png)

## Editing Terms & Conditions

The editor supports full **rich text formatting** including headings, bold, italic, lists, links, and raw HTML.

### Steps to update

1. Go to **Settings → Terms & Conditions**.
2. Click inside the editor area.
3. Write or paste your terms content.
4. Use the toolbar to format text (headings, lists, links, etc.).
5. Click **Save** to publish changes immediately.

:::info
The Terms & Conditions page is publicly accessible without login. Changes take effect as soon as you save.
:::

## HTML Support

The editor accepts raw HTML. Switch to the HTML source view to paste pre-formatted legal content directly.

```html
<h2>Acceptance of Terms</h2>
<p>By accessing or using eStay, you agree to be bound by these terms...</p>

<h2>User Responsibilities</h2>
<p>You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account...</p>
```

:::warning
Review your terms and conditions with a legal professional to ensure they are enforceable and compliant with local laws.
:::
