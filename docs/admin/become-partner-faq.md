# Become a Partner FAQ

Manage the FAQ content shown on your platform's public **"Become a Partner"** marketing page — the page prospective property owners see before they sign up. This is separate from your platform's general customer-facing FAQs.

:::info Multi-mode only
This page exists only when the platform is running in [Multi Mode](./overview.md#single-mode-vs-multi-mode).
:::

---

## Topics and FAQs

Content is organized into **topics** (e.g. "Getting Started", "Payouts & Commission"), each holding one or more **FAQs** (question + answer).

![Become a Partner FAQ](/images/panel/becomepartnerfaqstep1.png)

**Add Topic** — set a title (the slug auto-generates from it and can't be changed later) and a short description, up to 500 characters.

**Add FAQ** (within a topic) — a question (up to 500 characters) and an answer (up to 2000 characters).

:::warning Deleting a topic deletes its FAQs
There's no separate "move FAQs out first" step — deleting a topic removes every FAQ under it too. The confirmation dialog calls this out; read it before confirming.
:::
