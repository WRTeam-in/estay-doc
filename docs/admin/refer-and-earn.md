# Refer & Earn

The **Refer & Earn** program (under **Marketing**) rewards customers for inviting their friends. When a new customer signs up using a referral code and completes a qualifying booking, **both** people earn a reward coupon. This page lets you configure the reward rules and track every referral.

:::info Note
This is a **global** program — it applies platform-wide, not per country. The country and property switchers are hidden here.
:::

## How It Works

- Every customer has a personal **referral code**.
- A **referrer** shares their code; a **referee** (the new user) signs up with it.
- When the referee completes a qualifying booking, the platform issues reward **coupons** to both, according to your rules.

---

## Step 1: Open Refer & Earn

In your admin dashboard, go to **Marketing > Refer & Earn**. At the top you'll see overall stats:

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total Referrals** | Everyone who signed up via a referral. |
| **Successful Referrals** | Referrals that converted into a reward. |
| **Total Rewards** | How many reward coupons have been issued. |

## Step 2: Configure the Reward Rules

Click **Edit Reward Rules** to set up the program:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Program Status** | Turn the entire referral program on or off. |

Then configure each side separately:

### Referrer (the person who invites)

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Reward Percentage** | The reward the referrer earns, as a percentage (1–100%). |
| **Is Expirable** | Whether the referrer's reward coupon expires. |
| **Expiry Days** | If expirable, how many days until it expires (1–500). |

### Referee (the new user)

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Reward Percentage** | The reward the new user earns, as a percentage (1–100%). |
| **Is Expirable** | Whether the referee's reward coupon expires. |
| **Expiry Days** | If expirable, how many days until it expires (1–500). |

**Defaults:** Referrer 10% (expires in 90 days), Referee 15% (expires in 30 days).

![Edit Reward Rules](/images/panel/referstep2.png)

---

## Step 3: Track Referrals

The page has two tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Referee Rewards** | Rewards earned by new users who were referred. |
| **Referrer Rewards** | Rewards earned by existing users who referred someone. |

Each row shows the referrer, the referral code, the referee, the reward coupon, the related booking (if any), the **status**, and the referred date.

**Status meanings:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | The referral converted and the reward was issued. |
| **Pending** | The referee signed up but hasn't completed a qualifying booking yet. |
| **Expired** | The reward window passed without conversion. |

You can **search** by referrer and **filter** by status or whether a booking was made.

:::info
Referrals are kept in history even if a customer later deletes their account — such entries are marked **(Account deleted)** so your totals stay accurate.
:::
