# Reviews

Guest reviews left on the partner's properties, plus a way to flag one for removal.

---

## All Reviews

Every published review, with the guest's name, linked booking, rating, review text, and the property's overall average rating. Filter by status (Published / Removed). Click a row to see the full review, including any photos the guest attached (how many photos a guest can add is a server setting admin controls — see [Image Upload Limits](/docs/admin/image-upload-limits)).

![All Reviews](/images/partner/reviewsstep1.png)

---

## Requesting a Removal

A partner can't delete a review themselves — they can only **request removal**, with a required reason (e.g. "Abusive Language") and a description. This sends the review into a moderation queue admin decides on — see [Review Removal Requests](/docs/admin/review-removal-requests) for that side of it.

---

## Remove Requested

Tracks every removal request the partner has submitted and its outcome: **Requested** (awaiting admin decision), **Approved** (removed), or **Rejected** (stays published). Click a row to see the review alongside the reason and description originally submitted.
