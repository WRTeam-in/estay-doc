# Becoming a Partner

A partner's account starts on your platform's **public website**, not in the Partner Panel itself. The entry point is the **List Your Property** button in the site header:

![List Your Property button on the public website](/images/partner/becomepartnerstep1.png)

That leads to a "Become a Partner" landing page explaining the process, with a **Start listing now** button that opens the actual sign-up form:

![Become a Partner landing page](/images/partner/becomepartnerstep2.png)

The sign-up form itself collects just the basics: name, email, and password, verified with an email OTP. There's no country or property type to choose yet at this point.

Once that basic account exists, the new partner is sent to the **Partner Panel login page** to sign in for the first time.

:::info What this means for you
There's nothing to configure here — this step happens on the customer-facing website. What you do control is everything that comes next: the [Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.md) that runs the moment they log in, and the fields it asks for. See [Partner Registration Fields](/docs/admin/partner-registration-fields) on the admin side to customize what's collected.
:::
