# Login & Password Reset

## Logging In

Partners sign in at the Partner Panel login page with either **email** or **phone** (a toggle switches between the two) plus their password.

:::warning Suspended accounts are blocked at login
If admin has suspended the partner (see [All Partners](/docs/admin/all-partners)), login fails immediately with a message explaining the account is suspended — they never reach the panel.
:::

Right after logging in, a partner who hasn't finished setup or isn't yet Approved is sent straight to the [Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.md) — see that page for what determines whether they land there or on their normal Dashboard.

---

## Resetting a Forgotten Password

1. **Enter email** — the partner enters the email address on their account.
2. **Verify OTP** — a 6-digit code is emailed; entering it correctly moves to the next step. A resend option is available once the cooldown passes.
3. **Set a new password** — choosing and confirming a new password logs them straight in.
