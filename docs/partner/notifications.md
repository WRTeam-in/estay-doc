# Notifications

The partner's notification inbox — reached from the **bell icon** in the topbar, not the sidebar. Shows every in-app notification received (new bookings, cancellations, payouts, and more), unread ones marked with a filled bell icon. Delete a notification to dismiss it.

:::info Not the same as Settings
This is the inbox itself. Whether these notifications get sent by **email** as well is controlled separately, in [Settings](./settings.md).
:::
