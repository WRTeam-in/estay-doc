# Profile

Reached from the **user menu** (top right), not the sidebar — where a partner manages their own account details, separate from anything property-related. It's one continuous page, not separate screens: a Personal Info card, then Owner Address, Registration Details, and Change Password stacked below it.

![Profile Page](/images/partner/profilestep1.png)

---

## Personal Info

Avatar, name, phone, and email, shown at the top of the page. Editable anytime.

:::warning Changing email requires verification
Update the email field and the platform immediately sends verification codes — one to the current address, one to the new one. Both codes have to be entered correctly before the change takes effect; the email doesn't switch on its own just by editing the field.
:::

---

## Address

The partner's own address (separate from any property's address) — shown read-only with an **Edit Address** button that opens the edit form.

---

## Registration Details

An inline form, right on the page — re-save the custom [Partner Registration Field](/docs/admin/partner-registration-fields) answers submitted during the [Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.md) — useful if something needs correcting outside of a formal admin correction request.

---

## Password

Also inline, below Registration Details — current password, new password, and confirm, with their own **Update Password** button.
