# Today's Check-ins & Check-outs

Two focused lists for the day's front-desk work — trimmed-down views of [Bookings](./bookings.md) filtered to just today.

---

## Today's Check-ins

Confirmed and Checked-in bookings with a check-in date of today. Same booking info/customer/room/dates/payment columns as the main Bookings list. Filter by status (Confirmed / Checked-in).

The **edit** action here does two things at once, in order:

1. If payment is still outstanding (a Pay At Property booking), record the cash or UPI payment collected.
2. Move the booking to **Checked-in** — this opens the same mandatory room-selection step as on the main Bookings page; the guest isn't marked checked in until specific rooms are assigned.

**Download** gets the invoice.

![Today's Check-ins](/images/partner/todayscheckinsstep1.png)

---

## Today's Check-outs

Checked-in and Completed bookings with a check-out date of today. The **edit** action only appears on bookings still Checked-in, and simply marks them **Completed**.
