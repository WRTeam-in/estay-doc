# About Us Page

Control the content shown on the app's **About Us** screen.

## Multi Mode

1. Log in to your **Admin Panel**.
2. Navigate to **Content Management → About Us Page**.
3. Add the content.
4. Click **Save**.

The app fetches this content at runtime and renders it on the About Us page.

## Single Mode

About Us page opens from the **Discover More** tap in the Amenities section of the Home screen.

There are 3 sections on the About page, fully configured from the admin panel:

1. Log in to your **Admin Panel**.
2. Navigate to **Content Management → About Us Page**.
3. Fill the data in **Who We Are**, **Manage Key Highlights**, and **Our Promise** sections.
4. Click **Save**.

All 3 sections display together on the app's About Us page.
